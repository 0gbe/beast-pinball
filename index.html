  • Skill shot: plunge into the blinking E-Y-E lane.
  • E-Y-E lanes: light all three to raise the bonus multiplier (flip to change lanes).
  • The Crew: hit all four toys (Hypno, Brim, Hood, Racc) to light Beast Multiball at the eye scoop. Ramps score jackpots.
  • Drop targets: knock all three to light a Mission at the scoop. Finish all four for Beast Mode.
  • Combos: chain ramps and the left orbit within 4 seconds.
  • The Void: reach 250,000 and you get teleported to the bonus table for 19 seconds. Then it's 1M, 2.5M, 5M…
  • Wake the Beasts: each toy wakes up when you hit it or the shot next to it (Hypno: left orbit, Brim: right ramp, Hood and Racc: their inlanes). Wake all four for a Beast Party jackpot.
  • Eye Mood: the eyeball gets focused on a 2X combo and goes into a red frenzy at 3X.
  • Today's Challenge: a new goal every day. Finish it for a bonus and a spot on today's board.
  • Kickback in the left outlane, ball save at the start of every ball.
