# Changelog

## v1.8 — 2026-11-27 (Sleepy Eye, Neon Sputter, Next Rival, Millionaire Wink)
Four of today's ideas, approved by Ogbe. Physics, collision geometry, scoring rules, the v1.7 painted playfields, street-art HUD, the 4 small AKCB toys, the 19-second Void with its countdown ticks and the v1.6 sound mix are unchanged.
* Sleepy Eye: cradle the eyeball on a raised flipper for 8 seconds and its eyelids slowly droop (about 2.4 s to half-closed). The next flip (the other flipper, or re-flipping within half a second of letting it roll) snaps it wide awake with a "WAKE UP!" callout, a bright pluck and +10,000 (doubled like everything else in 2X modes and The Void). It resets with no bonus if the ball rolls away, drains, the game is paused or tilted. The normal awake eye is pixel-identical to before.
* Neon Sputter: each tilt warning makes the neon trim (HUD border and pills, the table's edge trim, the apron neon lines, the insert lights and flasher domes) stutter on and off like a failing street sign for 0.9 s, with a new original buzz-and-crackle sound tucked under the warning honk, then it steadies. It stops instantly on pause, full TILT, a new ball or game over.
* Next Rival: the game-over screen shows one small pill under your score with the score just above yours on the high-score board and how close you came (e.g. "12,400 SHORT OF BRIM"), "#1 NEW HIGH SCORE!" when you beat the top score, or "TIED WITH ... FOR #1". It fits with no overlap or scrolling on desktop, iPhone 13, iPhone SE and Pixel 5, and name entry and SHARE ON X work as before.
* Millionaire Wink: the first time your score passes 1,000,000 in a game, the eyeball turns to look at you and gives one slow, cheeky wink (0.8 s) with a soft sparkly "ting". Once per game; purely visual, so physics and Eye Mood are untouched.
* Sound: 3 new original sounds (sputter, wakeup, wink) designed in `tools/sound/design.py` and mastered like the rest (no clipping); every existing v1.6 sound is byte-identical. `tools/sound/add_sounds.py` adds sounds without re-encoding the others.

## v1.7 — 2026-11-27 (visual polish: painted playfields, street-art HUD)
Art direction: the bar set by @akidcalledbeast's game (rain-slicked streets, glossy reflections, crisp grounded shadows, bold street-art HUD). Layout, physics, collision geometry, scoring and the v1.6 sound are unchanged.
* Painted playfields: the main table is now a high-resolution painted rain-soaked street (neon puddles, lane lines, a painted metal-rimmed eye) and The Void is a painted obsidian-water vortex with bioluminescent cyan, magenta, violet and gold currents. Both are optimized JPEGs (`assets/playfield-main.jpg` 70 KB, `assets/playfield-void.jpg` 91 KB, from `tools/optimize-playfield-art.py`) embedded in `js/assets-data.js`, so the single-file publish copy needs no extra requests. The painted eye's pupil sits right under the VOID insert (EXTRA BALL on its lower lid) with a subtle cyan ring around it; the Void's golden vortex sits right behind the giant eyeball. They load before the first frame; until then (or if one ever failed) the table falls back to plain dark asphalt / obsidian and redraws once the image arrives.
* Cleaner zones: the procedural centre eye, box-junction grid, zebra crossing, dashed paint, road lettering, spray decals, puddles, grain and the Void's procedural ripples / spiral / rings are gone, so the painted art breathes. High contrast is kept for the active parts: subtle wet reflections only under the bumpers, slingshots, ramps and toy pads, and soft grounded drop shadows under ramps, rails, gates, bumpers, slingshots, posts, flippers, drop targets and the toys; a light depth vignette.
* New street-art HUD replaces the orange dot-matrix look: a charcoal score box with a thick neon border (cyan in play, violet in the Void and for callouts), a huge bold italic score with a dark outline and lime glow, slanted pill tags (BALL 1 in cyan, VOID 250K / missions / JACKPOTS in magenta, MULTIBALL! or 2X BEAST in the middle), lime /// motion ticks and a graffiti crown. Callouts (BEAST PARTY!, SKILL SHOT!, JACKPOT!, MULTIBALL...) appear on a tilted hot-magenta brush-stroke banner with the detail line in a wide pill. The Void shows THE VOID (magenta), 2X SCORE and a live pulsing countdown that flashes red in the last 5 seconds. Attract mode cycles BEAST PINBALL + BY OGBE, PRESS START, high scores, the eyeball, REACH 250,000 and Today's Challenge. On phones with big touch buttons the small side-panel label is hidden so nothing overlaps. (The 128x32 display still runs underneath, unseen, so nothing in the game logic changed.)
* Hardware kept from the first pass: pickup-style pop bumpers (magenta, lime, cyan) with chrome skirts, glossy caps and bloom; tinted acrylic ramps with chrome rails and screws; chrome rails and gates, rubber-ringed posts, street-art slingshots with under-glow, a deep neon-rimmed scoop; sculpted flipper bats; glowing toy pickup pads that pulse on Wake-Up / Beast Party; beveled glass inserts with neon bloom; reactive cyan / magenta / lime flashers; ball contact shadow + gloss (Eye Mood untouched); neon apron panels with the Today's Challenge pill; The Void's golden-eye halos, chrome core ring and the non-overlapping countdown.
* Menus: charcoal panels with cyan borders and cyan / lime ringed pause and sound buttons (same sizes and layout). "by Ogbe" / "BY OGBE" stay the game credit only.
* Faster than v1.6: cached sprites instead of per-frame gradients and blur filters. Busy 3-ball scene (`tests/v17-perf.mjs`, 3-run average, v1.6 -> v1.7): desktop render 30.0 -> 12.0 ms per frame on the main table and 18.8 -> 13.3 ms in The Void; iPhone 13 18.8 -> 7.5 ms / 10.6 -> 7.7 ms; live 30 -> 60 fps.

## v1.6 — 2026-11-26
* Totally original sound: every sound in the game was replaced by a new, cohesive set designed and rendered from scratch with our own synthesis code (`tools/sound/`). No samples, presets, sound libraries or third-party audio. The sound is wet and watery (bubbles, drips, plinks), with vinyl-toy knocks and squeaks and punchy MPC/SPD-style drums.
* 38 game sounds (61 files with variations) covering flippers, bumpers, slings, drops, spinner, lanes, ramps, scoop, toys, Beast Wake-Up, Beast Party drum, multiball, jackpots, the Void teleport, the Void ticks, the heartbeat, drain, ball save, launch, Today's Challenge, high score, tilt and the menu buttons. Plus a new music engine: a swung boom-bap groove for the main table and a half-time watery groove for the Void, sequenced live from the rendered kit, so it never loops exactly.
* Mixed and mastered: every sound is loudness-matched per category, cleaned with EQ, gently compressed and limited to -1.5 dBTP (each file is -1.0 dBTP or lower after encoding). In the game there are category buses, music ducking under big moments, a shared reverb, stereo placement by table position, small pitch/level changes on every hit, voice limits, and a master glue compressor, limiter and soft clipper, so a 4-ball multiball pile-up never clips. A rendered gameplay capture measures -16.3 LUFS integrated with a -2.5 dBFS peak.
* The spoken callouts (browser text-to-speech voices, which aren't ours) are retired. Stingers now carry those moments: combo, lit, mission, beast mode, ball saved, challenge complete, welcome to the Void.
* Sounds are embedded as base64 Opus with an MP3 fallback for Safari and older iOS (1.3 MB). They are decoded at page load, and iOS audio still unlocks on the first tap.

## v1.5 — 2026-11-25
* Heartbeat Drain: when the ball heads for the drain (the moment the eyeball's pupil shrinks in fear), a soft synthesized heartbeat plays and the table edges pulse a dim red. At most one beat every 0.9 s. It fades out quickly when the ball is saved, and stops when it drains, on pause and at game over. The plunger lane never triggers it.
* Share Your Score: a SHARE ON X button on the game-over screen opens a ready-to-post X message (x.com/intent/post) with your score, today's challenge name and whether you beat it, and the game link. Phones use the system share sheet when available and fall back to the X link. Works with mouse, touch and keyboard (Enter/Space on the button) and doesn't touch the name entry or the high score list.
## v1.4 — 2026-11-25
* Eye Mood: the eyeball's iris follows your combo streak. Calm (the classic look) normally, a focused electric-blue iris on a 2X combo, and a bloodshot red "frenzy" glow at 3X and up. It eases back to calm a couple of seconds after the combo ends.
* Beast Wake-Up: each of the 4 toys on the main table wakes up (glows, brightens and hops) when you hit it or the shot right next to it: Hypno = left orbit, Brim = right ramp, Hood = left inlane, Racc = right inlane. Wake all 4 for a BEAST PARTY: dot-matrix callout, synthesized drum hit, 75,000 (+25,000 for each further party in a game, up to 200,000). The toys go back to sleep after the party and at the start of every ball.
* Today's Challenge: one goal per day, picked from the date (Puerto Rico time) out of 10 (Ramp Rush, Void Walker, Wake the Beasts, Bumper Bash, Spin Doctor, Drop Zone, Toy Tapper, Combo Craze, Multiball Mania, Half a Million). Shown on the title screen and the dot-matrix attract loop, with a progress line on the apron while you play. Finishing it gives +100,000 and a callout. Games that finish it go on a "Today's Challenge" board (name, score, time to finish), saved on this device. A global Supabase board is optional and off by default.
